Our story

From fixing everyday IT to building connected systems.

We began by solving the problems organizations face every day: websites, computers, network connections and hands-on technical support.

Along the way we kept meeting the same frustrations: disconnected spreadsheets, paper records, manual approvals and systems that didn't talk to each other. So we grew into custom software, digital platforms, infrastructure and security, and turned the most common needs into our own Silo products.

Today we work with schools, universities, faith-based organizations, NGOs, public institutions and businesses, looking at the whole system rather than one piece of it.
