Support FAQ

Quick answers

I can't sign in to my Silo product.

Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. If that doesn't work, your administrator can reset your account, or submit a request above.

Can you train our new staff?

Yes. Training can be arranged under your support agreement or as a separate engagement.

How do I request a new feature or report?

Submit a request with the "Normal" priority and describe what you need. We'll scope and quote it.

Do you support systems you didn't build?

Often, yes. We can assess third-party or in-house systems and propose a support or co-development arrangement.
