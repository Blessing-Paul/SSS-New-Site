Partner FAQ

Before you apply

Is there a fee to become a partner?

No application fee. Commercial terms, such as margins, revenue share or service rates, are agreed during onboarding.

Who owns the code on a co-development project?

You do. Ownership, licensing and confidentiality are set out in the agreement before work begins.

Do partners receive training?

Yes. Resellers and implementers get product, sales and technical training plus access to demo environments.

How long does review take?

We aim to respond within a few working days with next steps or questions.
