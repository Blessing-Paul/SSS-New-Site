Questions

What people ask about Office Silo

Can approval routes match our hierarchy?

Yes. Routes, levels and escalations are configured to your structure and can differ by department.

Does it replace our accounting software?

Office Silo handles budgets, requests and payment records and can connect to your accounting system; full accounting is scoped separately.

Can branches see only their own data?

Yes. Access can be limited by branch, department and role.

Can we start with just one module?

Yes. Many organizations start with approvals or HR and add modules over time.
