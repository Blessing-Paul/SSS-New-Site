Ask us a question
What does Stable Shield Solutions do?

We provide technology solutions that may include custom software, websites, portals, desktop and mobile applications, IT infrastructure, cloud, networking, cybersecurity, payment integration and process automation.

Can you build a complete management system?

Yes. A project can range from a website to an application platform with user accounts, dashboards, workflows, payments, reporting and integrations, depending on the agreed requirements and scope.

Can your solutions run on our local servers?

Solutions can be designed for on-premises, cloud or hybrid deployment when the application architecture and support arrangements permit. The right approach depends on your needs.

Can you integrate online payments?

Payment integrations can connect supported third-party providers to suitable websites, applications and portals. Provider eligibility, payment methods and fees depend on the selected provider.

Do you support organizations outside education?

Yes. We work with businesses, government and public institutions, universities, healthcare, NGOs, agriculture and faith-based organizations, with each project scoped to its requirements.

Do you provide support after deployment?

Maintenance and technical support can be included through an agreed arrangement. Support hours, response times, coverage and service levels are confirmed in the applicable agreement.

Which Silo product is right for us?

We have 14 Silo products across seven categories, from our flagship School Silo to Business, Office, Revenue, Payment, Health and Automobile Silo. Use one on its own or connect several. Compare the products or book a demo and we'll help you choose.
