You might be wondering

Questions about the products

Do we need to buy several products?

No. Each Silo works independently. Start with the one you need most and connect others later as your processes grow.

Can you adjust it to the way we work?

Yes. Each product is configured to your structure and policies, and we can build extra modules or integrations as a custom project.

Can it run on our own server?

Depending on the product and your needs, we can host it in the cloud, on your premises, or a mix of both. We'll recommend what suits you.

What happens to our existing records?

We import them for you from spreadsheets or your previous system during setup.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the products, number of users, modules, hosting and support level. After your demo we'll send a clear proposal.

Can we resell or implement Silo products?

Yes. Join our partner programme as a reseller or implementation partner.
