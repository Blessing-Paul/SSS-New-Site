What you get

Everything School Silo does for you.

Every feature is configured around your policies. In your demo we'll confirm exactly what's included for your setup.

Admissions without the queue

Online application forms, document uploads, entrance exam scheduling, offers and acceptance, with applicants turned into student records automatically.

Results in minutes, not weeks

Teachers enter continuous assessment and exam scores; grading, ranking, comments and report cards are calculated and published with your approval.

Fees you can follow

Fee structures by class and term, discounts, instalments, online payment, receipts, overdue reminders and debtor reports.

Parents in the loop

A parent portal for attendance, results, homework, fee balances and school notices, with several children under one account.

A teacher's day, simplified

Class lists, attendance marking, lesson plans, homework and score entry, limited to the classes each teacher is assigned.

Exams & computer-based tests

Question banks, scheduling, automatic marking for objective questions and controlled result publication with audit trails.

Early years care

Check-in and check-out, authorized pickup contacts, daily activity notes and age-appropriate development reports.

School life

Timetables, calendars, events, clubs, transport, library and hostel records where enabled.

Oversight for leadership

Dashboards for enrolment, attendance, performance and fee collection, across one campus or many.

Questions

What people ask about School Silo

Can it follow our own grading and report format?

Yes. Grading systems, assessment weights, comments and report layouts are configured to your school's policies.

Do parents need to install an app?

No. The parent portal works in any phone browser.

Can parents pay fees online?

Yes, through an authorized payment provider, with receipts issued automatically.

Is our children's data safe?

Access is role-based, sensitive changes are logged, and backups are part of every deployment.
