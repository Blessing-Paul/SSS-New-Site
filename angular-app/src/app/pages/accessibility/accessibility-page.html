Our approach

This website uses semantic headings, keyboard-operable navigation, visible focus styles, responsive layouts, labelled form fields, light and dark themes and respects reduced-motion preferences.

Standards

We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA, and we test with keyboard-only navigation, screen readers, zoom and contrast tools.

In the products we build

We apply the same principles to client websites, portals and Silo products, so the people who use them can do their work comfortably.

Feedback

If something on this site is hard to use, please tell us through the contact page and we will work to fix it.
